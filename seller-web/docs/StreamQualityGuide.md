# Auqli Stream Quality Settings Guide

## Easy Quality Adjustments for Every Stream

As an Auqli streamer, you have complete control over your stream quality. Our intuitive quality settings let you balance visual clarity with data usage based on your needs and internet connection.

## Accessing Your Stream Quality Settings

1. **Before Starting a Stream**: Click the ⚙️ Settings icon on the pre-stream screen
2. **During a Stream**: Tap the Quality Settings button in your stream controls

## Understanding Quality Presets

For quick and easy setup, choose from our pre-configured quality presets:

| Preset | Best For | Data Usage |
|--------|----------|------------|
| **Low (240p)** | Poor connections, text-based content | ~120MB/hour |
| **Standard (480p)** | Most streams, balanced quality | ~300MB/hour |
| **High (720p)** | Detailed products, better quality | ~650MB/hour |
| **Premium (1080p)** | Professional streams, showcasing details | ~1.2GB/hour |

Simply tap the preset that works best for your needs and connection quality!

## Fine-Tuning Your Stream (Advanced)

Want more control? You can customize individual settings:

### 📺 Video Settings

- **Resolution**: Controls how clear your video appears
  - Higher values (e.g., 1920×1080) = Sharper video but more data
  - Lower values (e.g., 640×480) = Less sharp but saves data
  
- **Frame Rate**: Controls how smooth motion appears
  - Higher values (e.g., 30fps) = Smoother motion but more data
  - Lower values (e.g., 15fps) = Less smooth but saves data
  
- **Bitrate**: Controls overall video quality and data usage
  - Higher values (e.g., 2500 Kbps) = Better quality but more data
  - Lower values (e.g., 500 Kbps) = Lower quality but saves data
  
- **Mirror Mode**: Flips your camera view horizontally
  - On = What you normally see in your selfie camera
  - Off = What others see (good for showing text or products)

### 🎤 Audio Settings

- **Audio Quality**: Controls clarity of your voice
  - Standard = Clear voice quality with minimal data
  - High = Slightly better audio but uses more data

## Quick Tips for Better Streams

1. **Test Before Going Live**: Use our preview mode to check how your stream looks with different settings
2. **Match to Your Content**: 
   - Showing detailed products? Use higher resolution
   - Just talking to viewers? Lower resolution works fine
3. **Check Your Connection**: Our connection meter shows if your internet can handle your selected quality
4. **Monitor Data Usage**: Keep an eye on the data usage meter to manage your usage

## Quality Indicator

During your stream, you'll see a small quality indicator showing:
- Current resolution
- Frame rate
- Bitrate
- Estimated data usage per hour

You can drag this indicator anywhere on your screen or hide it completely.

## Why Auqli's Default Settings Work Best for Most Users

Our default settings (480p resolution, 15fps, 400-600 Kbps bitrate) are carefully optimized to:
- Work reliably on most internet connections
- Provide clear video for product demonstrations
- Use minimal data (within the range of 300mb to 378.88MB per hour)
- Balance quality and accessibility for your viewers

Start with these settings for your first streams, then adjust based on your specific needs!
